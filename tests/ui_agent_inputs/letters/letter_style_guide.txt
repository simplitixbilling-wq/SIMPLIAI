Formatting Rules:
- Use a formal business-letter structure.
- Include date, recipient line, subject line, salutation, body, closing, and signature block.
- Prefer short paragraphs over bullet-heavy prose unless the brief explicitly asks for a list.
- Do not invent facts, names, dates, or legal language beyond the briefing note.
- If any critical input is missing, show a square-bracket placeholder.

Tone Rules:
- Be professional, clear, and concise.
- For audit letters, maintain a cooperative and courteous tone.
- For HR letters, be firm but not aggressive.

Signature Rules:
- End with "Yours faithfully," unless the brief implies another conventional closing.
- Put the signatory name and designation on separate lines.
